Explaination of current design: 
- base plate: as the name suggests, this just acts as a supporting piece
- magnet hold: screws onto the base plate and is a tight friction fit with the magnet itself
- vca bobbin: sits atop the magnet and has minimal clearance with it, this is what the coil wounds over. This also has a small protruding cylindrical face - this is used so that the vca can be used with integration with any other device (in this case, LVDT)
- lvdt core: a long thin tube that holds the ferrite core for the LVDT. One side is threaded on the inside such that it can sit atop the protruding part of the vca bobbin. From the other side, it's hollow for about 10cm so that the ferrite piece can fit in.
- lvdt bobbin: a very thin layer that will be wounded by 3 sets of coil for the lvdt
- end plate: supporting piece on the other side. Has a hole in the middle to keep the linear bearing as a friction fit
- outer body: can be screwed onto the base plate and the end plate, and acts as an outer covering for the entire system
