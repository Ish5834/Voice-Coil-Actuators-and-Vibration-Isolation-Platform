The basic principle for the working of a voice coil actuator is the Lorentz force. it involves a magnet and a coil around the magnet. The coil is then given current which helps it generate a magnetic field. This field interacts with the magnetic field of the magnet which in turn gives way to Lorentz force. Either the coil or the magnet are held stationary, and hence the free one is free to move along the axis perpendicular to the plane of both the fields

In this project, we have held the magnet stationary (screwed onto the base plate/magnet hold) and hence the coil experiences a force.
